# Routines

Recurring tasks. The assistant edits this file when the owner asks for a new routine or a change, and schedules the
ones due today at the start of every conversation (scheduled tasks do not survive restarts).
Format: `- HH:MM (days) — what to do`

- At the start of every conversation: nothing extra yet.

- 08:00 (every day) — Morning summary on Telegram, short and in this order: 1) today's calendar (time and place,
  clashes, anything that needs preparing; one line if tomorrow starts early); 2) items in notes/todo.md due today or
  overdue; 3) email from the last 24 hours, urgent or needing a reply first, the rest grouped one line per email,
  promotions ignored. Make clear these are the new ones, not the whole inbox. Offer drafts where a reply is needed.
